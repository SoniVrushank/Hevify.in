## 4. How the site is already optimised

**On-page SEO**
- Unique, keyword-led `<title>` and `<meta description>` on every page.
- One clear `<h1>` per page; logical H2/H3 structure.
- Canonical tags, Open Graph and Twitter cards.
- Internal linking between homepage and all six blogs (and blog-to-blog "Keep reading").
- Fast, self-contained, mobile-first pages (no heavy frameworks).

**Structured data (schema.org)** — the biggest AEO lever
- Homepage: Organization, LocalBusiness, Person, Service ×3, FAQPage.
- Each blog: BlogPosting, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage.
- Keep the LocalBusiness NAP (name, address, phone) identical everywhere.

**AEO / GEO specifics**
- Every page opens with a direct "Quick answer" / plain-language summary — the exact style AI engines quote.
- FAQ blocks written as clean Q&A that LLMs can lift verbatim.
- Consistent entity description ("performance marketing and social media agency in Ahmedabad") repeated across pages, social profiles and directories so AI corroborates it.

## 5. Your action checklist (off-site — do these next)
These make the biggest difference and can't live in the code:

1. Create/claim **Google Business Profile** for Hevify Labs, Ahmedabad — same NAP as the schema. Add categories, photos, weekly posts.
2. Keep name, address, phone, and the one-line description **identical** on the website, Instagram (@hevify.in), LinkedIn, Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART.
3. Collect **genuine Google reviews** steadily; reply to each.
4. Publish **one new blog every 1–2 weeks** answering a real customer question (reuse the blog template — copy any blog file, swap the content and schema).
5. Earn **mentions** in local listicles / directories describing you the same way — this is what makes AI confidently recommend you.
6. Add real project results/case studies as you get permission — proof strengthens both trust and rankings.
7. Replace placeholder details before launch: real domain, favicon.webp, email (hello@hevify.in), and confirm the WhatsApp number.
