07 — Answers

Questions people ask us first.

What does Hevify Labs do?

Hevify Labs is a performance marketing and social media agency in Ahmedabad. We run paid ads on Meta and Google, manage social media and content, handle SEO and GEO for AI search, and build AI automations that save your team time.

Where is Hevify Labs located?

We're based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, and work with clients across India and globally — most of our delivery is remote-first.

How much does it cost to work with Hevify Labs?

Packages start at ₹6,000/mo for SEO, ₹8,000/mo for performance marketing and ₹10,000/mo for social media. Ad spend and hosting are billed separately, so you keep full control of your budget.

What is GEO and do I need it?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity recommend your brand when people ask them questions. As search shifts toward AI answers, GEO works alongside traditional SEO to keep you visible.

How soon will I see results?

Paid ads can show signal within the first few weeks; SEO and GEO typically build over 3–6 months. We set clear expectations and report against the metrics that matter to your business.
