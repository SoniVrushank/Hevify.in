Complete Guide to Performance Marketing

By Hevify Labs · Ahmedabad

Performance marketing is the discipline of connecting ad spend directly to revenue — not impressions, not vague brand lift, but a number you can point to. If a rupee spent on Meta or Google Ads can't be traced to a rupee (or more) earned back, it's not performance marketing, it's just advertising.

What makes marketing "performance" marketing

Four things have to connect: the ad, the audience, the landing page, and the tracking. Most campaigns that underperform are missing one of these — a great ad sending traffic to a slow landing page, or a good landing page with no tracking to prove it worked.

The channels that actually move revenue

  • Meta Ads — best for reaching people who aren't actively searching yet, using interest and behavior targeting.
  • Google Ads — best for capturing people already searching for what you sell.
  • Retargeting — brings back visitors who almost converted, usually the highest-ROI spend in any account.

A campaign with a 2% click-through rate and a landing page that converts at 8% will beat a 5% click-through rate campaign sending traffic to a page that converts at 1%, every time.

How to know if it's working

Track cost per lead or cost per sale weekly, not monthly. Waiting a full month to check performance means a month of budget spent on something that might have needed fixing in week one.

Where most brands get stuck

Usually not the platform — the landing page and the follow-up. Traffic without a fast, clear next step is wasted spend, no matter how good the targeting was.

FAQ

What's the difference between performance marketing and regular advertising?

Performance marketing ties every rupee spent to a measurable result — a lead or a sale — instead of just tracking impressions or brand awareness.

Which platform should I start with, Meta or Google?

Start with Google if people already search for what you sell by name. Start with Meta if you need to introduce the product to people who aren't searching yet.

How much should a small business budget for performance marketing?

It depends on the industry, but starting with a defined test budget over 4-6 weeks is usually enough to see whether a channel is working before scaling it.
